An AI assistant that answers guests, your own direct-booking website, and a refresh of the property itself: furniture, paint and decoration. One-off price, with three months of adjustments included.

Smart home and security

The technology that lets a property run without someone on site. We install it, connect it to your booking calendar and keep it working.

Smart locks

A new door code for every booking, sent to the guest automatically and deleted at check-out. No keys to hand over or lose.

Noise monitoring

Sensors that measure volume only and never record conversations. We get an alert and act before the neighbours complain.

Leak sensors

An alert the moment there is water where there should not be, before it reaches the flat below.

Climate control

Air conditioning and heating switch off when guests leave and on before they arrive, so the bill only runs when someone is there.

Entrance cameras

At the door or gate only, never inside the property, as Airbnb rules and data protection law require.
